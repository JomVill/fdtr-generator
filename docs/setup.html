Weekly Schedule

First, open your class schedule. Log in to the MSU-IIT portal (opens in a new tab) and go to Academics › Class Schedule. Copy each class onto the calendar below, then add your consultation hours and any other work (admin, research) as Others.
  1. Add a time block: double-click an empty spot, or click and drag down. On a phone or tablet: double-tap, or press and hold, then drag.
  2. Edit it: click the block to choose what it is (Class, Consultation…), add a name like “CS 101”, and pick other days with the same time.
  3. Move or resize: drag the block, or drag its bottom edge.
Handy for keeping one schedule per semester.

Your details and schedule save automatically in this browser. To use them on another computer, use Backup & restore at the top.
